# TapShot

TapShot is a gallery shooter for the RayNeo X3 Pro smart glasses that distills the genre down to two inputs: drag to slide a laser cannon locked to the bottom of the screen, and tap to fire. Six waves cycle through goals borrowed from the shooters that defined the genre — a Space Invaders-style formation that clears and accelerates as it thins, a Galaxian-style breakaway dive, a Galaga-style rescue mission where shooting the wrong target costs the reward rather than a life, and a Missile Command-style defense wave. The pacing is deliberately relaxed rather than a bullet hell: enemy fire is rate-limited across the whole wave rather than per enemy, dives arrive one at a time, and reaching the bottom costs a life rather than ending the run outright.

## Controls

- Drag — slide the cannon
- Tap — fire
- Swipe — menu navigation only
- Back — quit a run
